ABOUT THE CHALLENGE

Where a sketch becomes a working prototype

MECHONOVATE is a 24-hour product development challenge hosted at Nadimpalli Satyanarayana Raju Institute of Technology (NSRIT) by the DEPARTMENT OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING, built for students who want to take up an idea from a rough sketch to a working model built under real time pressure.

Teams pick a theme, define a real-world problem inside it, and spend the next day and a half designing, prototyping, and defending their solution in front of a panel of engineers and faculty.

  • Hosted at NSRIT (Autonomous)

    HOW IT RUNS

    One continuous build, three stages

    Ideate

    Pick a theme, scope a real problem, sketch the concept and set your build plan.

    Design

    CAD, circuits, or code — get the design reviewed by mentors

    Develop

    Build, test, and iterate the working prototype, then present it to the judging panel.

    The 6 rounds across 24 hours
    Round 1Problem Identification1 hrs
    Round 2Ideation & Concept Development2 hrs
    Round 3Design & CAD2 hrs
    Round 4Fabrication & Prototype Development12 hrs
    Round 5Testing & Improvement3 hrs
    Round 6Final Presentation & Evaluation4 hrs
    RULES & ELIGIBILITY

    Before you sign up

    Eligibility

    Open to undergraduate engineering students.

    Team size

    2 to 5 members per team, from the same or different colleges.

    What to bring

    • Laptop and any personal tools
    • Valid college ID
    • Any pre-approved components (list to be confirmed)

    Judging criteria

    • Originality and theme fit
    • Technical execution and working prototype
    • Real-world feasibility and impact
    REWARDS

    What's on the table

    ₹0TOTAL PRIZE POOL
    • Certificates of participation and merit for every team member
    • Direct feedback from faculty and industry mentors on your build
    • Recognition through NSRIT's Institution's Innovation Council
